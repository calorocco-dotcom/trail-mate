# Device identity and sharing privacy

Trail Mate uses a personally held device identity for author operations. A
connected device proves possession and signs authorized Geocaching or trail
content. The browser is an editing tool; it does not issue a website account
that owns your identity.

## Independent services

Reticulum gateways provide network access. Content directories index and serve
shared publications. They may be operated independently and on different
nodes. Reaching a directory does not require making its machine your account
provider.

Author signatures let recipients check publication authorship independently
of a directory's display text. Directory proofs describe a directory result;
they are not a guarantee that every other directory stores the same content
or that a route is physically safe.

## What stays local and what becomes public

| Information | Visibility in the ordinary workflow |
| --- | --- |
| Device private identity material | Held by the device; ordinary author operations use signatures. Explicit identity backups are sensitive. |
| Unpublished browser drafts | Stored in the current browser until exported or published. |
| Unshared device recordings | Stored on the device/media; connecting USB lets the authorized tool read supported records. |
| Published KML, coordinates, time and metadata | Available to the selected directory and recipients of the public content. |
| Public author key and publication history | Can link publications made under the same author identity. |
| Gateway connection metadata | Observable to the gateway or network parties that provide that connection. |
| External map requests | Observable to the selected map provider. |

Independent operators and portable files reduce the need to give one platform
your entire private history. They do not prevent someone from saving or
analysing data you intentionally publish. A public route may reveal a home,
regular starting point or timing pattern if you include that information.

## Review before sharing

Check the route, waypoints, recording times, images, descriptions and embedded
KML metadata. Publish only what you intend to disclose. Keep a private original
if you create a public edited copy. Changing website language or RF protocol
does not create a new author identity or make the old public history anonymous.

Archiving records a new publication state and can remove the item from ordinary
active results. It cannot remotely erase copies or past versions already held
by another directory or recipient.

## Identity custody

Keep backups protected and retain recovery information you need. Someone who
obtains usable private identity material can act as that author. A lost device
without a usable backup may mean losing the ability to maintain its published
content. These facts apply even though there is no central account login.

Trail Mate's privacy benefit is control over identity, local records and the
choice of sharing relationships. Its documentation does not promise that no
platform can ever learn a public route or infer behaviour from public data.
